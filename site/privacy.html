Privacy

What this site collects, what it doesn't, and how to reach us about it. Last updated October 26, 2026.

The short version

This site doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you across other sites and doesn't sell or rent your data to anyone. We count visits with cookieless analytics, and we only see your email address if you write to us.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how many people visit and which pages they read. It sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device and doesn't build a profile of you. We see aggregate numbers such as page views, referring sites, countries and device types, not individual visitors.

When you contact us

There's no sign-up or account on this site. You reach us by email at the address below; if we add a contact form later, this section covers it too. We use your email address and anything you include in your message only to reply and, if we work together, to run that project. We keep it only as long as we need it for that, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

We don't sell your data

We never sell, rent or trade personal information, and we don't use it for advertising. We only share it when we're legally required to.

Services this site relies on

  • Hosting: the site is served by GitHub Pages, which may log technical request data such as IP addresses for security and reliability.
  • Analytics: Cloudflare Web Analytics, as described above.

Our products, such as StrongPantry and Celestial Destiny, run on their own sites with their own checkout and privacy terms. Payments are handled by those checkout providers, and we never see your full card details.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it. Email us and we'll reply within 30 days.

Contact

Questions about privacy: corporate@thestrongautomation.com
