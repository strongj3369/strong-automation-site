Product questions

Do I need an account to try a product?

No. Celestial Destiny, SIGNAL and ColorTuneLyrics can be used free right away. Run Offline is a one-time download with no account at all.

How do I pay?

Each product has its own secure checkout. Celestial Destiny reports and StrongPantry plans are paid by card on their sites, and Run Offline is purchased through Etsy.

Does Run Offline really work without internet?

Yes. It's a single file that runs in your web browser and keeps your data on your own device.
